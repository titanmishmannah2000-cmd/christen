1 | Paul urges Timothy to stop false teachers. “Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners; of whom I am chief.” || “Christ Jesus came into the world to save sinners, of whom I am chief” [[bible:1 Timothy 1:15]]. Paul uses his own life to show God’s patience and mercy: even those who spoke against Jesus can change [[bsn:1 Timothy 1:12-20]].
2 | Pray for everyone, including rulers. “There is one God and one mediator between God and men, the man Christ Jesus.” Guidance on men and women in worship. || How teaching and authority are related here depends on how both are understood; women did teach (Titus 2:3–5) [[tosn:1 Timothy 2:12]]. The New Testament keeps differences between men and women while proclaiming equality in Christ (Galatians 3:28) [[tosn:1 Timothy 2:13]].
3 | Qualifications for overseers and deacons: above reproach, faithful in marriage, gentle, not lovers of money. An early hymn: “God was revealed in the flesh.” || God’s household is “the assembly of the living God, the pillar and ground of the truth” [[bible:1 Timothy 3:15]] [[tosn:1 Timothy 3:15]]. The list of leaders’ qualities is like the one in Titus 1 [[bsn:1 Timothy 3:1-16]].
4 | Some will abandon the faith. “every creature of God is good.” “Let no man despise your youth; but be an example.” || “Bodily exercise has some value, but godliness has value in all things” [[bible:1 Timothy 4:8]] (compare 1 Corinthians 9:24–27) [[tosn:1 Timothy 4:8-9]].
5 | Care for widows; honour elders; don’t accept accusations without witnesses. || The church was expected to care for widows among its members (Deuteronomy 10:17–19; James 1:27) [[tosn:1 Timothy 5:3-16]]. Timothy was to treat other believers like family [[bsn:1 Timothy 5:1-6:2]].
6 | “Godliness with contentment is great gain.” “The love of money is a root of all kinds of evil.” “Fight the good fight of faith.” Command the rich to be generous. || “We brought nothing into the world, and we certainly can’t carry anything out” [[bible:1 Timothy 6:7]]. True contentment is resting in Christ as all we need [[tosn:1 Timothy 6:6]].
