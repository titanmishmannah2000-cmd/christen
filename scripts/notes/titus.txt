1 | Paul tells Titus to appoint elders in every town on Crete: blameless, hospitable, holding firmly to the truth. || “One of them, a prophet of their own” is Epimenides of Knossos, a Cretan who lived around the 500s BC. Greek writers called Cretans liars for claiming to have the tomb of Zeus [[tosn:Titus 1:12]]. Against this Paul sets “God, who can’t lie” [[tosn:Titus 1:2]].
2 | Teach older and younger men and women, and slaves, to live well. “The grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all men,” training us to live godly lives as we wait for “our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ.” || “Our great God and Savior, Jesus Christ” [[bible:Titus 2:13]] is one of the few places where the New Testament plainly calls Jesus God (see also John 1:1; 20:28) [[tosn:Titus 2:13]].
3 | Be ready for every good work. God saved us “not by works of righteousness… but according to his mercy, he saved us through the washing of regeneration and renewing by the Holy Spirit.” || 3:4–7 may sum up or quote a teaching the church already shared (compare 1 Timothy 1:15) [[tosn:Titus 3:4-7]].
