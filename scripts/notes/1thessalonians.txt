# sourced
1 | Paul remembers the Thessalonians’ “work of faith and labor of love and perseverance of hope”: they turned from idols to serve the living God. || Paul thanks God for their faith, love and hope (compare 1 Thessalonians 5:8) [[tosn:1 Thessalonians 1:3]]. The Holy Spirit convinced them that the message was true [[tosn:1 Thessalonians 1:5]].
2 | Paul recalls his gentle ministry among them, “like a nursing mother”, and their suffering for the gospel. || Paul and his companions worked with their hands to support themselves (Acts 18:1–4), so as not to be a burden [[tosn:1 Thessalonians 2:9]] [[bsn:1 Thessalonians 2:1-16]].
3 | Timothy brings good news of their faith, and Paul prays they will abound in love. || Unable to come himself, Paul sent Timothy, because he was worried for this young church [[tosn:1 Thessalonians 2:1-3:13]] [[tosn:1 Thessalonians 3:1-5]]. They were already known for their love for one another [[tosn:1 Thessalonians 3:12]].
4 | Live holy lives and work quietly. Those who have died in Christ will rise first when the Lord comes, “so we will be with the Lord forever. Therefore comfort one another.” || Paul writes so that they will not “grieve like the rest, who have no hope” [[bible:1 Thessalonians 4:13]]. Jesus’ death and resurrection are the ground of that hope [[tosn:1 Thessalonians 4:14]], and all his followers will live with him for ever [[bsn:1 Thessalonians 4:13-18]].
5 | The day of the Lord comes like a thief, so stay awake. “Always rejoice. Pray without ceasing. In everything give thanks.” || The breastplate and helmet recall Isaiah 59:17 and Ephesians 6 [[tosn:1 Thessalonians 5:8]]. “Always rejoice. Pray without ceasing” [[bible:1 Thessalonians 5:16-17]].
