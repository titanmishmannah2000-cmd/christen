# sourced
1 | Peter writes to God’s chosen “foreigners” scattered abroad. God has given a living hope through the resurrection, an inheritance that cannot fade. Though tested by fire, faith is precious. “Be holy, for I am holy.” || The Old Testament prophets spoke of this salvation long before it came, and even the angels long to understand it [[tosn:1 Peter 1:10-12]]: “which things angels desire to look into” [[bible:1 Peter 1:12]].
2 | Come to Christ, the living stone; you are living stones, “a chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation”. Live honourably among outsiders. Christ suffered for you, leaving an example. || “A chosen race, a royal priesthood, a holy nation” [[bible:1 Peter 2:9]]: Peter gives Israel’s titles (Exodus 19:5–6) to his mostly Gentile readers, showing they are truly God’s people [[tosn:1 Peter 2:9]]. “He himself bore our sins in his body on the tree” echoes Isaiah 53 [[tosn:1 Peter 2:24]].
3 | Wives and husbands; don’t repay evil for evil. “Always be ready to give an answer… with humility and fear,” Christ suffered once for sins, the righteous for the unrighteous. || Christ “preached to the spirits in prison” [[bible:1 Peter 3:19]] has been read three ways: the dead after his resurrection (which the rest of the New Testament does not support), the people of Noah’s day through Noah, or rebellious spiritual beings to whom the risen Christ announced his victory [[tosn:1 Peter 3:19-20]].
4 | Since Christ suffered, arm yourselves with the same mind. “Love covers a multitude of sins.” Don’t be surprised at fiery trials; share Christ’s sufferings. || “Good managers of the grace of God in its various forms” [[bible:1 Peter 4:10]]: the variety of spiritual gifts God gives his people [[tosn:1 Peter 4:10]].
5 | Elders, shepherd God’s flock willingly. “casting all your worries on him, because he cares for you.” The devil prowls like a roaring lion; resist him. || “Babylon” was probably a symbolic name for Rome, like Babylon a great capital of an empire [[tosn:1 Peter 5:13]]. Silvanus is also known as Silas [[tosn:1 Peter 5:12]]. Peter calls himself a fellow elder [[tosn:1 Peter 5:1-4]].
