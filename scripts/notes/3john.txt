1 | The elder writes to his friend Gaius, praising his hospitality to travelling missionaries, warning about Diotrephes, “who loves to be first”, and commending Demetrius. “Don’t imitate that which is evil, but that which is good.” || “I have no greater joy than this: to hear about my children walking in truth” [[bible:3 John 1:4]]. Gaius welcomed the travelling teachers who went from church to church [[tosn:3 John 1:5]], who went out “for the sake of the Name”, meaning Jesus Christ [[tosn:3 John 1:7-8]]. Diotrephes, by contrast, lorded it over others [[bsn:3 John 1:1-15]].
