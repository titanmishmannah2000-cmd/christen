# sourced
1 | Paul is astonished that the Galatians are turning to “a different ‘good news’”. His gospel came by revelation of Jesus Christ, not from people. || In his other letters Paul gives thanks or praise after the greeting; here he goes straight to a rebuke [[tosn:Galatians 1:1-5]]. For how he came to follow Jesus, see Acts 9:1–19 [[tosn:Galatians 1:15-16]]. Being Christ’s servant means staying true to Christ, however others react [[tosn:Galatians 1:10]].
2 | The Jerusalem leaders accepted Paul’s mission to Gentiles. Paul opposed Peter in Antioch for withdrawing from eating with Gentiles. “I have been crucified with Christ… Christ lives in me.” || Cephas is the Aramaic for “rock”, Peter in Greek [[tosn:Galatians 2:11]]. When Peter drew back from eating with Gentile believers, his actions would have weakened the message of salvation by grace through faith [[tosn:Galatians 2:12]].
3 | Did you receive the Spirit by works of the law or by faith? Abraham believed God. Christ became a curse for us. “There is neither Jew nor Greek… neither male nor female; for you are all one in Christ Jesus.” || Abraham’s story shows that faith has always been the way to receive God’s promises [[tosn:Galatians 3:6-9]]. In Christ, everyone comes to God and receives his promises in the same way, and believers form one body (compare Genesis 1:27; 1 Corinthians 12:12–13) [[tosn:Galatians 3:28]].
4 | God sent his Son “when the fullness of the time came” so we might be adopted as children, crying “Abba, Father!” The allegory of Hagar and Sarah. || The word translated “tutor” in 3:24 is paidagōgos: in the Greco-Roman world, a trusted servant who trained and protected an heir until he grew up [[tosn:Galatians 3:24]]. So God’s people were under the law of Moses until Christ came [[tosn:Galatians 4:1-3]].
5 | “Stand firm therefore in the liberty by which Christ has made us free” Use freedom to serve one another in love. Walk by the Spirit: “the fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith, gentleness, and self-control.” || “The fruit of the Spirit is love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faith” [[bible:Galatians 5:22]]. The works of the flesh come from trying to live without God’s Spirit [[tosn:Galatians 5:19]]; the Spirit leads people to follow Jesus’ example [[bsn:Galatians 5:13-26]].
6 | Bear one another’s burdens. “Whatever a man sows, that he will also reap.” Paul writes in large letters with his own hand: “a new creation” is what matters. || “Far be it from me to boast, except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ” [[bible:Galatians 6:14]]. Boasting in keeping the law would take away from the cross [[tosn:Galatians 6:14]]; what matters is the new creation Jesus brought [[bsn:Galatians 6:11-18]].
