1 | Paul remembers Timothy’s sincere faith, which lived first in his grandmother Lois and mother Eunice. “God didn’t give us a spirit of fear, but of power, love, and self-control.” Don’t be ashamed. || The faith “which lived first in your grandmother Lois, and your mother Eunice” [[bible:2 Timothy 1:5]] gave Timothy a grounding in the Old Testament (Acts 16:1–3) [[tosn:2 Timothy 1:5]] [[bsn:2 Timothy 1:1-18]]. Paul and the elders had laid hands on him for his ministry [[tosn:2 Timothy 1:6]].
2 | Be strong in grace; endure like a soldier, an athlete, a farmer. “If we are faithless, he remains faithful.” Rightly handle the word of truth. || “Commit the same things to faithful men, who will be able to teach others also” [[bible:2 Timothy 2:2]]: guarding the faith includes handing it on [[tosn:2 Timothy 2:2]]. Paul’s suffering, like Christ’s, helped bring salvation to others [[tosn:2 Timothy 2:8-13]].
3 | Hard times will come in the last days. Continue in what you learned: “Every Scripture is God-breathed and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for instruction in righteousness.” || “Every Scripture is God-breathed” [[bible:2 Timothy 3:16]]: it comes from God’s own speech, and God stands behind his word (compare 2 Peter 1:20–21) [[tosn:2 Timothy 3:16]]. God’s Spirit uses Scripture to teach and heal [[bsn:2 Timothy 3:1-4:5]].
4 | “Preach the word.” Paul’s farewell: “I have fought the good fight. I have finished the course. I have kept the faith.” He asks for his cloak and books, and Mark. || Paul sees his coming death as a drink offering poured out to God [[tosn:2 Timothy 4:6]]. “Take Mark… for he is useful to me” [[bible:2 Timothy 4:11]]: Paul and Mark, once parted (Acts 15:37–39), were reconciled [[tosn:2 Timothy 4:11]]. Winter stopped sea travel, so Timothy should come soon [[tosn:2 Timothy 4:21]].
